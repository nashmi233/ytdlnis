<b>Hammel · حمّل</b>

Download video and audio from supported links with an Arabic Android interface. Share a link directly to Hammel to start downloading a video, or paste a link in the app to explore available formats.

• Direct link sharing from other apps.
• Clipboard link detection and optional automatic download.
• Download queue, progress and history.
• Choose folders and show downloaded files in the gallery.
• TikTok video, audio and cover choices for links opened inside the app.
• Black interface and accessible quick controls.
• Backup and restore settings and data.

Availability, formats and quality depend on the source and yt-dlp support. Download only content you have permission to save.
